# General Requirements

MediaVore is a media tracking application designed for cinephiles and TV show enthusiasts. It leverages the TMDB (The Movie Database) API to provide up-to-date information on millions of titles.

## Core Purpose
The app allows users to:
- Discover new and trending movies and TV shows.
- Search for specific titles, actors, or directors.
- Track their viewing progress (especially for TV series).
- Manage personal watchlists and custom lists.
- Earn achievements based on their interaction with media.
- Receive notifications for upcoming content.
