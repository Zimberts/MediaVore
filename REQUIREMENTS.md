# MediaVore Requirements

This document provides quick access to the detailed requirements and explanations for each feature of the MediaVore application.

- [**General Overview**](requirements/general.md): An introduction to MediaVore's purpose and core functionality.
- [**Theming & Design System**](requirements/theming.md): Requirements for color consistency and the multi-theme architecture.
- [**Home Row & Navigation**](requirements/navigation.md): Explanation of the persistent navigation bar and global UI elements.
- [**Discovery & Search**](requirements/discovery.md): Detailed look at the media browsing grid, search (with debounce), and filtering systems.
- [**Media Details & Actors**](requirements/media_details.md): Breakdown of the information and actions available for titles and actor profiles.
- [**Saved Media (My Lists)**](requirements/saved_media.md): Requirements for list management, display modes, and social sharing features.
- [**Seen History & Logging**](requirements/seen_history.md): How viewing history is logged and organized.
- [**Media Statistics**](requirements/statistics.md): Visual insights into viewing habits and metrics.
- [**Notification Center**](requirements/notifications.md): Tracking upcoming releases and quick-adding next episodes.

## System Features

- [**Settings**](requirements/settings.md): Configuration options for appearance, gaming, and data management.
- [**Achievements**](requirements/achievements.md): Information on the gamification system and milestone tracking.
